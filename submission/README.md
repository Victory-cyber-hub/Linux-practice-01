# Submission

The uploaded activity specifies final checks and an optional command-history bonus. It does not specify a separate submission format.

Before submitting, review the original instructions from your instructor/class in case they provide additional requirements outside the uploaded task.

## Recommended Items to Review

- Completed `project` directory in Kali Linux.
- Final `tree project` output.
- Final `ls -la` permission checks.
- Screenshots/evidence if requested by your instructor.
- Optional `commands_used.txt` if completing the bonus.

## Important

Do not replace your actual results with placeholders when submitting the completed work.

The files below are templates/placeholders for organizing your own results:

- `command-history.txt`
- `final-output.txt`
